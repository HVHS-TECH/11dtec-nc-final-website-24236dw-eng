How football was created

I will be giving you information about how football was created and how it has changed over the years.

Football was started in China around 206 BC-220 AD and was called cuju meaning (kick ball). They used animal bladder as balls (usally pig bladder) and had 12-16 players per side and was played on rectangular fields like as it is today.

football spread to England where they invented football around 1100-1300s. They played with hunderds of people from villages and there were many casualties from injuries to even death. It was mostly played on relgious holidays like Shrove Tuesday.

Later football was banned in England in the 13th century. Where 3 of the main bans came from the government which were in 1314, 1331, 1388 as the sport was being used hurt people rather enjoying the game.

Football was secrelty being played since the bans and was played in priavte schools using sticks as goals. Some rules were rugby and some were football rules. Football was played as a sport rather than a way to show dominace. As rules kept getting evoloved in Britan, schools strated to make clubs.

In 1848 the Cambridge rules were established in Cambridge University meaning your werent forced to pass the ball backwards like you would in rugby. This started the foundation of football rules.

In October 26th 1863 the Football Association was created in England and they made the first official rules of football. They banned the use of hands and made it so you can pass the ball forward and sperated football from rugby.

The first ever international game played was in 1872 on 30th November at Hamilton Crescent and was against Scotland and England, where the score was 0-0.

As the British love for football was seen by the world other countries started making leagues like:

  • Neatherlands and Denmark in 1889
  • Argentina 1893
  • Chile, Belgium and Switzerland in 1895
  • Itaily in 1898
  • Germany and Uruguay in 1900
  • Hungary in 1901
    • France in 1903 Finland in 1907.

In 1885 players were now getting payed rather than playing for free as revenue increased of specators watching the games.

In 1904 the FIFA (Federation Internationale de Football Association) was created in Paris, France and they were the ones who organized the first world cup in 1930.

Football has evolved so much since it was created from being used as a way to show dominance to now being the most popular sport in the world with over 4 billion fans. It has become a global phenomenon and continues to grow.

Leagues

There are many football leagues around the world such as the Premier League in England, La Liga in Spain, Serie A in Italy, Bundesliga in Germany, Ligue 1 in France. There are also like the Major League Soccer in the United States all around the world. These leagues feature some of the best teams and players in the world and are followed by millions of fans worldwide.

Premier League: Premier League is fasted paced and is very high intensity demanding you to go back and fourth across the pitch.

La Liga: La Liga is more technical and is more focused on skill and ball control, they try to build up the game.

Serie A: Serie A is more tactical and is more focused on defense and strategy. It is more important to defend than attack.

Bundesliga: Bundesliga is more physical and is more focused on strength and athleticism. It is more important to be strong than skillful.

The change of balls

Throughout the years, footballs have been changed a lot. From being made out of leather to synthetic materials, the ball has evolved to provide better performance and durability.

In the early days of football, balls were made out of leather and were often filled with animal bladders. These balls were heavy and could become waterlogged, making them difficult to play with.

In the 1900s, Richard Lindon introduced rubber bladders and better hand switched balls.

1950s-1970 they introuduced the first perect sphere football.

1960s-1980 synthetic materials were used to create more consistent and durable balls.

In the modern day balls are made from a variety of materials and are designed to provide optimal performance in different weather conditions.
