How football was created

I will be giving you information about how football was created and how it has changed over the years.

Football was started in China around 206 BC-220 AD and was called cuju meaning (kick ball). They used animal bladder as balls (usally pig bladder) and had 12-16 players per side and was played on rectangular fields as today.

But as football spread across the globe it went to England where they invented football in 1863. They played with hunderds of people from villages and there were many casualties from injuries to even death. It was mostly played on relgious holidays like strove Tuesday. Later football was banned in England in the 14h century.

Football was reintroduced in the 19th century and it was played in schools and universities. In 1863, the Football Association (FA) was formed in England, which standardized the rules of the game and helped to popularize it. The first official international football match was played between Scotland and England in 1872.
