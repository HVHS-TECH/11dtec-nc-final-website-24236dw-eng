World Cup

All the latest news and updates about the World Cup

The first ever world cup was held in 1930 in July 13 to July 30 by Uruguay. The tournament has been held every four years since then, except during World War II. The most successful team in the history of the World Cup is Brazil, with five titles to their name. The current champions are France, who won the tournament in 2018.
