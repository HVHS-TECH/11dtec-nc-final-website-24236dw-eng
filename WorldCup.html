World Cup History

The first ever world cup was held in 1930 in July 13 to July 30 by Uruguay by Fifa. The world cup gets held every four years since then, except during World War II. Every country that is a member of Fifa can compete in the world cup, but only 32 teams can make it to the final tournament. The world cup is one of the most watched sporting events in the world, with billions of people tuning in to watch the games.

World Cup 2026

This year, the World Cup is being held in Qatar, making it the first time the tournament has been held in the Middle East. The tournament is scheduled to take place from November 21 to December 18, 2022, and will feature 32 teams from around the world competing for the coveted trophy.

World Cup Winners

Teams that have won the world cup are:

Brazil won it 5 times in 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, and 2002

Germany won it 4 times in 1952, 1974, 1990, and 2014

Italy won it 4 times in 1934, 1938, 1982 and 2006

Argentina won it 3 times in 1978 and 1986 and 2022

France won 2 times in 1998 and 2018

Spain won 1 time in 2010
