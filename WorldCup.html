World Cup

All the latest news and updates about the World Cup

The first ever world cup was held in 1930 in July 13 to July 30 by Uruguay by Fifa. The world cup gets held every four years since then, except during World War II. The most successful team in the history of the World Cup is Brazil, with five titles to their name. The current champions are France, who won the tournament in 2018.

This year, the World Cup is being held in Qatar, making it the first time the tournament has been held in the Middle East. The tournament is scheduled to take place from November 21 to December 18, 2022, and will feature 32 teams from around the world competing for the coveted trophy.

Teams that have won the world cup are Brazil in 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, and 2002; Germany in 1952, 1974, 1990, and 2014; Italy in 1934, 1938, 1982, and 2006; Argentina in 1978 and 1986; France in 1998 and 2018; Spain in 2010..
